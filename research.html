Research study

Bandit Portfolio Selection

Implemented and compared UCB and epsilon-greedy multi-armed bandit policies for daily portfolio selection across 30 U.S. equities, stress-tested through the March to April 2020 COVID market shock. Tested longer holding periods and EMA reward updates, and found epsilon-greedy with those enhancements most robust in the volatile regime. The comparison is reported straight, including where UCB committed too early and lost.

Master's capstone

Regime-Aware Pairs Trader

A Markov-switching regime filter tested on the XLE/XOP energy ETF pair across the 2018 oil selloff and the COVID crash, with cointegration tests, VaR backtests, and a stress analysis. Reported straight: the pair was not cointegrated and neither strategy was profitable, but the filter cut the maximum drawdown. An honest null result.
