Flagship

QuantRisk

Fixed-income and credit-risk analytics platform

A daily risk pipeline that turns a bond and credit portfolio into the numbers a risk desk acts on: valuations and key-rate durations, VaR and Expected Shortfall, a named-scenario stress engine, and a credit PD model with macro stress. About 4,800 lines of Python, 37 passing tests, an HTML report and a Streamlit dashboard, with Docker, CI, and Terraform. Runs on sample data, or real FRED Treasury curves when configured.

Capstone

Regime-Aware Pairs Trader

Master's capstone, Financial Engineering

Does pausing a pairs trade during high-volatility regimes help? A Markov-Switching filter tested on XLE/XOP across the 2018 oil selloff and the COVID crash, with cointegration tests and VaR backtests. Reported straight: the pair was not cointegrated and neither strategy made money, but the filter cut the drawdown and sat out the crash. An honest null result.
