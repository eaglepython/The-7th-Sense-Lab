Flagships — working software

Flagship

QuantRisk

A daily fixed-income and credit-risk pipeline: valuations and key-rate durations, VaR and Expected Shortfall, a named-scenario stress engine, and a credit PD model with macro stress. ~4,800 lines of Python, 37 passing tests, an HTML report and a Streamlit dashboard, with Docker, CI, and Terraform. Runs on sample data, or real FRED Treasury curves when configured.

Capstone

Regime-Aware Pairs Trader

Does pausing a pairs trade during high-volatility regimes help? A Markov-Switching filter tested on XLE/XOP across the 2018 oil selloff and the COVID crash, with cointegration tests and VaR backtests. Reported straight: the pair was not cointegrated and neither strategy made money, but the filter cut the drawdown and sat out the crash. An honest null result.
