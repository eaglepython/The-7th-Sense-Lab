Capstone

Regime-Aware Pairs Trader

Master's capstone, Financial Engineering

Does pausing a pairs trade during high-volatility regimes improve risk-adjusted performance? A Markov-Switching regime filter tested on the XLE/XOP energy ETF pair across the 2018 oil selloff and the COVID-19 crash, with cointegration tests, VaR backtests, and a stress analysis. I reported it straight: the pair was not cointegrated and neither strategy was profitable, but the filter cut the drawdown and sat out the crash. An honest null result.

How I work

Three habits behind every project.

Rigorous methods

Econometrics, statistical tests, and validation. I check whether the assumption holds before I trust the result.

Honest reporting

I report results even when they are null or inconvenient. A clean negative finding is worth more than a dressed-up one.

Reproducible

Tests, documentation, and code someone else can run. If it cannot be rerun, it is an anecdote, not a result.
